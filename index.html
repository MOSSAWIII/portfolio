About

Financial analysis, engineering and research

I work as a Finance Business Partner at Attijariwafa bank, focusing on financial analysis, reporting, strategic analysis and decision support. Previously I worked as a Data Consultant with OCTO Technology (Accenture), on projects including an event-driven recommendation engine.

My academic training includes a State Engineering degree in Data Science from INSEA Rabat and a Master's degree in Machine Learning for Data Science from Université Paris Cité. My current interests lie in robust financial ML, uncertainty, transparent methodology and reproducible research.

Public projects shown here are personal or shareable technical work samples; they do not disclose confidential employer data.
